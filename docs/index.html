Bendi Go

An open-source Roblox game where kids learn Israeli traffic rules by driving, and earn a star for every rule they follow.

How it works

Drive, follow the rule, earn a star

Instead of memorizing rules from a book, players get behind the wheel of Bendi, a friendly blue car, and drive around a small Israeli neighborhood. Every time they handle a traffic situation the right way, they earn a star. Bendi is built by Ben & Dima, a father-and-son team.

🚗

Drive

Explore a town with streets, sidewalks, shops, and parked cars.

🚦

Meet a rule

A red light, a speed sign, someone at a crosswalk, or a roundabout.

✋

Do it right

Stop, slow down, or give way, just like a real Israeli driver.

⭐

Earn a star

Mistakes never cost stars. Bendi gives a friendly tip instead.

🚦 Stop at red, go on green 🔄 Give way in the roundabout 🚸 Let pedestrians cross ⏱️ Keep to the speed limit 🛑 Stop fully at stop signs

PRD-001

Drive around town

Give players a car they can drive around a small Israeli neighborhood. Driving should be fun on its own, because everything after this needs players who want to drive.

  • Your own Bendi car: blue, big eyes, and a yellow "BENDI GO" plate.
  • Drive with the keyboard, a gamepad, or touch pedals on a phone.
  • A town with streets, sidewalks, buildings, trees, and parked cars.
  • A "Reset car" button if you get stuck.
All 12 requirements

Game

  1. Every player gets their own Bendi car: blue, with big eyes and a yellow "BENDI GO" license plate.
  2. Drive with the keyboard (W/S or arrows to drive and reverse, A/D to steer, Space to brake), a gamepad (triggers and left stick), or touch (on-screen pedals and steering).
  3. The top speed is about 70 km/h, so the 50 km/h town limit means something later.
  4. The camera follows the car from behind and a little above.
  5. One small neighborhood: at least 4 blocks and 4 crossings, two-way streets with right-hand traffic, lane markings and sidewalks, buildings, trees, and parked cars, walls at the map edges, and space saved for a roundabout.
  6. A "Reset car" button puts the car upright on the nearest road, facing the way traffic goes.
  7. Players' cars never collide with each other.
  8. One fixed scale for every speed: 1 stud is 0.28 m.

Project setup

  1. A Rojo project maps the source folders into Roblox.
  2. Pinned versions of Rojo, StyLua, and Selene, installed with a toolchain manager.
  3. Unit tests run outside Roblox Studio and fail if line coverage drops below 95%.
  4. CLAUDE.md and README.md explain how to build, sync, lint, format, and test.

PRD-002

Traffic lights and stars

The core learning loop: see a rule, follow it, earn a star. Traffic lights come first because they are the easiest rule for a kid to understand.

  • Real Israeli traffic lights at the crossings, with a white stop line.
  • A star for stopping at red and waiting for green.
  • A star counter, and friendly tips from Bendi in a speech bubble.
  • Bendi explains flashing green and red with yellow the first time.
All 10 requirements
  1. At least 3 of the 4 crossings get traffic lights, each with a white stop line.
  2. Lights go red → red and yellow → green → flashing green → yellow, and crossing directions are never green at the same time.
  3. Green lasts about 15 seconds, flashing green 3, yellow 3, and red and yellow 2. All of it is set in one config file.
  4. Stopping fully before the line at red and waiting for green earns a star. Running a red gets a tip. Crossing on yellow is never counted as a mistake.
  5. Each light gives a player a star at most once a minute, so circling one crossing doesn't work.
  6. A star counter is always on screen, with an animation and a sound for every new star.
  7. Tips show as a speech bubble from Bendi for about 4 seconds.
  8. Bendi explains flashing green and red and yellow the first time a player sees them.
  9. All text is in English and Hebrew, following the player's Roblox language.
  10. Stars last for the session. Saving them comes in PRD-006.

PRD-003

Speed limits

Teach players to notice speed signs and keep under the limit, especially where people are around, like near a school.

  • A speedometer in km/h that shows the limit and turns orange when you're too fast.
  • 50 km/h town streets, and 30 km/h on a quiet street and near the school.
  • Israeli speed signs, plus a "school ahead" sign.
  • A star for driving a whole street within the limit.
All 9 requirements
  1. A speedometer shows the car's speed in km/h.
  2. It also shows the current limit, and turns orange when the car is over it.
  3. Town streets are 50 km/h, the Israeli default. A residential street and a school zone are 30 km/h.
  4. Each zone starts with an Israeli speed sign (a red circle with the number) on the right. School zones also get a "school ahead" sign and a road marking.
  5. Driving a whole street, from crossing to crossing, no more than 3 km/h over the limit earns a star. The margin keeps small bumps fair.
  6. Each street gives a player a star at most once a minute.
  7. Going more than 3 km/h over for more than 2 seconds shows a tip, at most once per street.
  8. The first time a player enters a 30 km/h zone, Bendi explains why some streets are slower.
  9. Waiting at a red light doesn't break a street or count against the player.

PRD-004

Pedestrian crossings

Add people walking around town, and teach players to stop and let pedestrians cross at crosswalks.

  • Zebra crosswalks, including one in the school zone.
  • Kids, adults, and an older person who walk and cross the street.
  • A star for stopping and letting them cross.
  • Nobody can ever be hit: pedestrians step back and the car slows down.
All 8 requirements
  1. At least 4 zebra crosswalks with crosswalk signs: at least 2 in the middle of a block, and 1 in the school zone.
  2. Pedestrians walk along the sidewalks, and someone crosses each crosswalk every 20 to 40 seconds.
  3. Pedestrians wait at the curb, look both ways, and never step out in front of a car that is too close to stop.
  4. Stopping before the crosswalk until the pedestrian reaches the other side earns a star. Driving onto it while someone is on it gets a tip.
  5. Each crosswalk gives a player a star at most once a minute.
  6. Pedestrians can never be hit. If a car gets too close, they step back to the curb and the car is gently slowed. There are no crashes or injuries.
  7. Kids, adults, and an older person walking slowly, each looking different.
  8. The first time a player meets a waiting pedestrian, Bendi explains the rule.

PRD-005

Roundabouts and yielding

Add other cars to the road, and teach players who goes first: at roundabouts, at stop signs, and at give-way signs.

  • Other cars drive around town and follow the same rules.
  • A roundabout with Israeli signs, where cars already inside go first.
  • A stop sign that needs a full stop, and a give-way sign.
  • A star for every time you give way the right way.
All 11 requirements

Other cars

  1. NPC cars drive fixed routes and follow the same rules the player learns: red lights, pedestrians, speed limits, and the roundabout.
  2. They keep a safe distance and slow down for the player. If a player blocks one, it waits instead of pushing.
  3. About 6 to 10 NPC cars drive at once, so the roundabout often has cars in it.

Roundabout

  1. A single-lane roundabout with 4 entrances, in the space saved in PRD-001.
  2. Each entrance has an Israeli roundabout sign, a give-way sign, and a painted give-way line.
  3. Cars drive around it counterclockwise.
  4. Waiting behind the line for a car already in the roundabout earns a star, and so does entering without stopping when it's clear. Making a car in the roundabout slow down gets a tip.

Stop and give-way signs

  1. One crossing has stop signs on its side street. A full stop before the line earns a star, and rolling through gets a tip.
  2. Another crossing has give-way signs. Letting cars on the main street pass first earns a star.
  3. Each entrance or sign gives a player a star at most once a minute.

Tips

  1. The first time a player reaches a roundabout, a stop sign, or a give-way sign, Bendi explains the rule.

PRD-006

Driving lessons and license

Turn free driving into a journey: short guided lessons that teach one rule at a time, saved progress, and a final test that earns a Bendi Go driving license.

  • Five short lessons, one for each rule, with arrows on the road and a mini-map.
  • Earn 3 stars to pass a lesson and open the next one.
  • A driving test, a license card with your name and avatar, and a badge on your car.
  • Stars, lessons, and the license are saved between visits.
All 11 requirements

Lessons

  1. One lesson for each rule: driving basics, traffic lights, speed limits, crosswalks, and roundabouts with stop and give-way signs.
  2. Each lesson is a 2 to 4 minute route, with arrows on the road, a mini-map, and Bendi explaining the rule.
  3. Earning at least 3 stars passes a lesson. Players can retry as often as they want.
  4. Lesson 1 starts on a player's first visit, each lesson unlocks the next, and free driving is always available.
  5. A lesson menu shows every lesson, whether it's locked or passed, and the best star count.

Driving test and license

  1. Passing all 5 lessons unlocks the driving test: one longer route with every rule.
  2. The test is passed by following every rule on it. Tips still show, so a failed test teaches something.
  3. Passing shows a celebration and a Bendi Go driving license card with the player's Roblox name, avatar, and the date, styled after the Israeli license.
  4. The player's car gets a "Licensed driver" badge that other players can see.

Saved progress

  1. Stars, passed lessons, best star counts, and the license are saved with Roblox DataStores.
  2. If saving or loading fails, the player keeps playing, the game retries later, and progress is never reset by a failed load.

PRD-007

Realistic graphics

Make Bendi Go look like a real town, with day and night, and let players drive from inside the car with the driving instructor next to them.

  • Realistic light, shadows, and distant fog, and a sky that turns from day to night.
  • Street lamps, headlights, and lit windows after dark.
  • Curbs painted the Israeli way, and detailed buildings, trees, and cars.
  • An inside view with a dashboard, a steering wheel, and the instructor.
All 20 requirements

Light and sky

  1. Roblox's Future lighting, with soft shadows, light fog in the distance, a soft glow around bright lights, and warmer colors.
  2. A whole day takes 20 minutes and comes from the server clock, so everyone sees the same sky: 11 minutes of day, a 2-minute sunset, a 4-minute night, and a 3-minute sunrise.
  3. The night is never too dark to drive.

Night lights

  1. Street lamps stand on both sides of the streets and shine on the road at night, clear of corners, crosswalks, and the roundabout.
  2. Every moving car turns on headlights at night. NPC brake lights keep working.
  3. At night, some windows in every building light up.

Streets

  1. Curbs are painted the Israeli way: red and white for no stopping (within 12 m of crossings, the roundabout, and crosswalks), blue and white for paid parking on the middle streets, and plain gray for free parking.
  2. Roads, sidewalks, and curbs use realistic materials, and sign and traffic light poles are metal.

Buildings, trees, and gardens

  1. Buildings have windows with roller shutter boxes, balconies with glass railings, a stone band at the bottom, a low roof wall, and solar water heaters.
  2. Round trees have fuller crowns, and palms have drooping fronds and dates.
  3. Gardens have low hedges along the sidewalk, with gaps to walk through.

Cars

  1. Every car has bumpers, tinted windows with a sloped windshield, a roof, mirrors, wheels with silver rims, headlights, taillights, and a yellow Israeli license plate.
  2. The Bendi car still looks like the mascot: blue, big eyes, and a "BENDI GO" plate.
  3. Cars drive and bump into things exactly like before. The new parts are only for looks.

Inside view

  1. Switch between the outside and inside views with a button, the V key, or the gamepad's right stick. The game remembers the choice.
  2. The driver sits on the left, with a glowing speedometer and speed limit sign, a clock screen that shows the game's time, air vents, a gear lever, a rear-view mirror, and a steering wheel that turns with the player's hands at 10 and 2.
  3. The instructor sits on the right. Look around with the right mouse button, Q and E, the right stick, or a finger, and the view turns back to the road on letting go.
  4. Other players see the instructor and the dashboard through the windows.
  5. The player's own character is hidden in the inside view, and the windows are clearer from inside.

Performance

  1. The game still runs smoothly on phones and tablets: lights cast no shadows, and Roblox's quality setting lowers detail on slower devices.

PRD-008

Brake lights

The Bendi car's taillights light up red when the player slows down or stops, just like a real car and like the NPC cars.

  • Bright red brake lights whenever you brake.
  • They stay on while you wait at a red light or a crosswalk.
  • You can see when other players' cars brake, too.
All 5 requirements
  1. Both taillights turn bright red while the car brakes: Space, the brake pedal, the gamepad, pressing back while still rolling forward, braking in reverse, or the game slowing the car for a pedestrian.
  2. They stay on while the car stands still with a driver in it, like holding the brake pedal at a red light.
  3. They stay off while speeding up, driving along, rolling with no pedal pressed, or reversing, and go off soon after the player stops braking.
  4. A Bendi car with no driver never has its brake lights on.
  5. Every player sees the same lights. Each player's game works them out from how the car moves, with no extra network messages and no flicker.

Rules for every milestone

What never changes

These five rules apply to every part of the game.

🇮🇱

Israeli rules, correctly

Cars drive on the right, speeds are in km/h, and signs, markings, and lights look and work like in Israel.

💛

Kind feedback

The game never takes stars away. A mistake gets a short, friendly tip that explains the rule.

🔤

Easy to read

All text is short, in English and Hebrew, and ready for more languages.

🛡️

Safe for kids

No extra chat, no purchases, and players' cars can't bump into each other.

🚀

Done means published

A milestone is done when it's on Roblox and Ben has played it and checked every point.
